About

Clearpath exists to help people build a practical path out of debt.

We focus on realistic tools and honest guidance for people dealing with real debt. No miracle promises. No get-rich shortcuts. Just practical help for moving forward.

Why Clearpath exists

Managing debt can feel overwhelming. Most people know they need a plan but struggle to stay organised, motivated, and consistent over the long term.

A lot of financial advice online assumes you already have discipline, extra income, and access to opportunities that simply do not exist for many households. It tells you what to do without acknowledging why doing it is hard.

Clearpath was built to take a different approach.

Through practical tools, clear educational guides, and honest information about debt repayment strategies, Clearpath helps individuals take control of their finances one step at a time. The belief at the heart of everything here is simple: financial progress does not require perfection. Small, consistent actions taken over time can create meaningful change.

Our flagship Debt Snowball Tracker helps users organise debts, see their debt free date, track progress month by month, and stay focused on becoming debt free. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is ever stored or shared.

Practical over theoretical

Every guide is written to be immediately useful, not just interesting to read.

Honest about difficulty

Getting out of debt is hard. We do not pretend otherwise or promise shortcuts.

Private by design

Your financial information is your own. Our tools never collect or store your data.

Free and accessible

The tools and guides are free. No paywalls, subscriptions, or accounts required.

Oluwaseun Busayo Amusan
Founder, Clearpath

Clearpath was founded by Oluwaseun Busayo Amusan, a fintech professional, product builder, and entrepreneur with direct experience building financial technology products for everyday people.

Busayo has spent years working within consumer finance and financial technology, most recently at YALO Technologies, a Nigerian fintech platform helping people access financial services that were previously out of reach. Working in that environment made one thing very clear: people do not lack the desire to manage their money better. They lack accessible, practical tools that work for their real situation.

Clearpath grew out of that observation. The goal was never to build the most sophisticated financial platform. It was to build the most useful one for people who are carrying debt and looking for a real way forward.

His broader work focuses on financial inclusion, product development, and building technology that makes financial management simpler and more human.

Our editorial principles

Every piece of content published on Clearpath is created with these principles in mind.

Accuracy over sensationalism. We do not publish content designed to alarm or impress. We publish content designed to be useful.

Practical advice over theory. Every guide should be actionable. Something you can do today, not just something interesting to know.

Honesty about limitations. We are not licensed financial advisors. Our tools are educational. We say so clearly and consistently.

Plain language throughout. Financial jargon is one of the things that makes personal finance feel inaccessible. We avoid it deliberately.

Continuous improvement. Content is reviewed and updated as information changes. If something here is wrong, we want to know. Email us at hello@clearpath.click.
