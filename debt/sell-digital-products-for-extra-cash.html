The Easiest Digital Products to Sell When You Need Extra Cash

Clearpath Team10 min read

Make it once. Sell it again and again. No inventory, no shipping, no second job. Here are the simplest digital products to start with and how to actually sell them this weekend.

This is not a get-rich guide. It is a get-a-bit-extra guide. Which is what debt payoff actually needs. Let me show you what works.

You are not trying to build a business right now. You are trying to find a few hundred extra dollars a month to throw at your debt. Digital products are one of the most realistic ways to do that without trading more hours for dollars.

You make the file once. Someone downloads it. You did not do anything extra. They can do it again tomorrow. And the day after. That is the whole model.

1. Printable planners and trackers

The easiest entry point. People are always searching for ways to organise their lives and a clean, well-designed planner gives them exactly that. Weekly planners, meal planners, budget planners, goal trackers. The demand never goes away.

You do not need design training. Canva has free templates that get you most of the way there. Bundle ten to twenty pages together, pick clean colours and typography, export as a PDF. Done.

2. Budget spreadsheets

People want to feel in control of their money. A simple spreadsheet that helps someone track expenses, plan a monthly budget, or calculate their debt payoff is genuinely valuable. The ones that sell best are not the most complex. They are the ones a stressed, busy person can open and immediately understand.

Ad space

3. Notion templates

Notion has become the go-to workspace for people who are tired of juggling five different apps. Clean templates for a finance dashboard, a content calendar, or a simple life-organising system sell steadily. If you already use Notion for yourself, you may already have the beginnings of something sellable.

4. Resume and cover letter templates

Job seekers need something that looks good and is easy to edit, right now. A clean, modern resume template solves that instantly. People buy these because they need them urgently, which means conversion rates are high.

Pick the category that matches something you already know or use. Making a debt tracker printable to sell? Start there. You already understand what someone needs.

5. Canva template packs

Small business owners and creators are short on time. Instagram post sets, Pinterest pin templates, business flyers. You make one template and sell it to hundreds of people. Nobody needs to know you spent an afternoon on it.

6. Short, focused ebooks or workbooks

You do not need a 200-page book. A focused 25 to 40 page guide that solves one specific problem can sell well. Add some fillable worksheets at the end and you have something that feels premium without a huge amount of extra work.

How to make your first one this weekend

>

Pick one problem you understand personally. Spend 20 minutes on Etsy looking at what already exists and reading the reviews. See what people say is missing. Build the simple version of that thing in Canva, Google Sheets, or Notion. Write your description around the outcome, not the features.

Your first version will not be your best one. That is completely fine. Get it done and out in the world. You learn more from one live product than from ten hours of planning.

"Your first product will not be your best one. That is the point. Get it out. Learn. Build the next one better."

Where to sell it and what to expect

Etsy is the easiest starting point because the buyers are already there. Setup takes an afternoon. Gumroad or Payhip cost less in fees once you have your own audience. Start with one platform and get comfortable before going wider.

On income: a $15 printable bundle selling 20 times a month is $300. Not life-changing, but real extra money you can send straight to your debt. Once you have that working, add a second product. Then a third.
