Common questions

Before you ask

Do you save my numbers anywhere?
No. The whole thing runs inside your browser. Nothing gets sent to a server, nothing is written to a database, and we could not see your figures even if we wanted to. Close the tab and it is all gone.
My lender quotes a monthly rate. What do I do?
Switch the toggle above from Per year to Per month, then type the monthly figure straight in. If your car finance is 2.5% a month, you enter 2.5. The maths adjusts on its own.
Snowball or avalanche, which should I pick?
If you have started a payoff plan before and then quit, go with snowball. Clearing a small debt fast gives you proof it is working, and that is usually what keeps people going. If you have never quit one and you want to save the most money, avalanche wins. Try both and see what the difference actually is for your numbers, because it is often smaller than people expect.
Should I include my mortgage?
Leave it out for now. Almost everyone handles the house separately, and putting it in here will make the date so far away that it stops being useful.
How accurate is the date?
It is solid, assuming your rates hold and you keep paying the same amount. Life will interfere at some point, so treat it as a target rather than a promise. Even so, having a specific month to aim at changes how the whole thing feels.
